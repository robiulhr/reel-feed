Reel Feed collector

Open a Facebook page's Reels tab.

How to use this
  1. On Facebook, open a page (for example a competitor's) and click its Reels tab.
  2. This panel lists the reels. Scroll the page to load more, or click Keep scrolling and it scrolls slowly for you.
  3. Narrow them down: Min views, Sort, Search. Press ▶ on a reel to watch it here and see what it is about.
  4. Tick the reels you want (or Select shown), then click Add to Reel Feed.
  5. Reel Feed opens in a new tab. There you choose categories and lists, for all of them or one by one, and click Add.

It only works on a page's Reels tab, and only reads what you can already see there. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click Add.
